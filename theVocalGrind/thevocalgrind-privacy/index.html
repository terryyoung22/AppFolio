Privacy Policy

Short version: TheVocalGrind does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything in the app stays on your device.

Overview

TheVocalGrind ("the App") is a lock screen widget application for iPhone that displays inspirational quotes, tracks daily goals, and maintains a usage streak. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

TheVocalGrind is developed and operated by an independent developer. Your privacy is important to us, which is why the App is built with a local-first architecture — meaning all data stays on your device at all times.

Data We Do Not Collect

TheVocalGrind does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, Apple ID)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Health or fitness data
  • Contacts, photos, or files
  • Financial or payment information
  • Any data from your lock screen or home screen

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally on your iPhone using Apple's standard UserDefaults and App Groups storage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server.

  • Your selected quote category and refresh interval preferences
  • Your daily goal text and whether it was marked achieved
  • Your quote favorties list
  • Your daily quote streak count
  • A history of your last 3 achieved goals

All of the above is stored exclusively on your device. Uninstalling the App removes this data completely.

Third-Party Services

TheVocalGrind does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or data collection services. There are no third-party services of any kind embedded in the App.

WidgetKit and Lock Screen Access

The App uses Apple's WidgetKit framework to display content on your iPhone's lock screen and home screen. WidgetKit is a native Apple framework. The App only reads data from its own local storage to populate the widget — it does not read any other content from your lock screen or device.

Children's Privacy

TheVocalGrind does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. As no data is collected from any user, the App complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated in the future, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the App, please reach out via the App Store support link on TheVocalGrind's App Store page.
